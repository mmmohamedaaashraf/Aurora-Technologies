Try to cheat the camera.

A simple pose tracker measures the arm against the edge of the picture, so leaning sideways makes the arm look higher. Axiora measures against the patient's own trunk, the way the International Society of Biomechanics defines it, and names the compensation.

Shihab

The exercise becomes a meteor shower.

Shihab shows patients their own body as light, with a path for where the arm should go. A clean repetition shatters a crystal into sparks.

A compensation doesn't cost points. The part of the body doing the cheating glows red, so the patient knows what to fix. We chose this because research on rehab games found that point penalties do little to reduce compensation.

On-screen instructions are written in Arabic first.

Click or tap the mirror to do a repetition. Every third one, the figure shrugs.
